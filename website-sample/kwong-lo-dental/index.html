About Dr. Kwong Lo

Dr. Kwong Lo and his caring team offer a wide variety of dental services for you and your family, serving Toronto, Hamilton, Dundas, Ancaster and Stoney Creek.

No matter whether the kids need a routine cleaning or mom needs a root canal, we can do the job.

One in downtown Toronto and two in Hamilton, with extended hours available.
