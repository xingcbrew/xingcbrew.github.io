About Soho Dental

Providing top-quality dental care in downtown Toronto since 2008, from a friendly team that is highly skilled and compassionate.

Our passion is to keep you smiling with confidence.

Two convenient downtown clinics

Spadina & Richmond in the garment district, and Bloor West near Bathurst Station in the Annex.

Relaxing, beautifully designed spaces

Exposed brick, post and beam, and restored art deco details blend a modern feel with the history of the building.

Experienced, advanced-trained dentists

Dr. Haji has devoted 500+ hours of continuing education to implant, esthetic and restorative dentistry.

Comfort-first, individual care

Comfortable, non-threatening dentistry, with root canals and extractions done gently and safely.
