About Me

Hi, I'm Xing, the designer and data nerd behind Xing Brew Design Co. I'm based in downtown Toronto, where I work as a freelance knowledge translation specialist and scientific and medical data visualizer. My background is in data science and graphic design, and I've co-founded a data science consultancy and consulted for organizations including WHO, the World Bank, UNICEF, and ISGlobal.

This site is my corner of the internet for design and digital products. Some are just for fun, like games and creative tools that make learning feel like play. Others solve real problems, like poster templates that save researchers hours, planners that keep busy households on track, and apps that make everyday handoffs a little easier.

Most of what's here started with something I needed myself, as a parent of three young kids or as someone who's spent years making complicated information easier to understand. I believe good design should make the complicated feel simple, whether it's a clinical guideline figure, a math game, or a handoff card for a babysitter.
