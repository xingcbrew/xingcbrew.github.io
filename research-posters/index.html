Questions

What poster sizes do you work with?

Any size your conference asks for. Common ones are 36×48 in, 42×56 in, 48×36 in (landscape) and A0. Send me the guidelines and I'll match them exactly.

How long does a custom poster take?

Usually about 3-5 business days from receiving all your content to the first draft, then 1–2 days per revision round.

What software do I need for the templates?

Each template lists what it works in, usually PowerPoint (Windows or Mac) or a free Canva account. No design software required.

What files will I get with the done-for-you service?

A print-ready PDF sized for your poster, plus the editable source file so you can reuse or update it later.

Can you create graphics and data visualizations for my poster?

Yes, as a data scientist/graphic designer, this is what I love to do! Extra charges apply for each custom visualization. Please include this in your request, and I will provide a quote.

Can you do a rush job?

Often, yes. Email me with your deadline and I'll tell you honestly whether it's doable. Rush fees may apply.

Can I get a refund on a template?

Templates are digital downloads sold through Etsy, so refunds follow the shop's policy on Etsy. If a file isn't working, message me there and I'll help.

Have a poster deadline coming up?

Email me with your conference, poster size, and deadline. I usually reply within 24 hours.
